# User Guide - Ten Main Controls

1. **Stock navigation button** - opens the Stock Overview page.
2. **History navigation button** - opens the Adjustment History page.
3. **Graph navigation button** - opens the Monthly Stock Graph page.
4. **About navigation button** - displays application, client, student and module information.
5. **Adjust Stock button** - opens the stock adjustment form from the dashboard.
6. **Product row** - opens the adjustment form with that product pre-selected.
7. **Add Stock option** - selects a positive stock adjustment.
8. **Remove Stock option** - selects a negative stock adjustment.
9. **Update Stock button** - validates and saves the transaction, including employee and reason.
10. **Apply Filters / Refresh button** - reloads the history list using the selected filters.

## Additional input controls

- **Search field** filters products by name or SKU.
- **Product selector** chooses the item to adjust or graph.
- **Reason selector** records why stock changed.
- **History product/type filters** narrow the audit trail.
- **Month selector** changes the period displayed on the graph.

## Typical stock update

1. Open **Stock** and tap a product, or press **Adjust Stock**.
2. Select **Add Stock** or **Remove Stock**.
3. Enter a quantity greater than zero.
4. Enter the employee in charge.
5. Select a reason.
6. Press **Update Stock**.
7. Read the confirmation message.
8. Open **History** to verify the audit record.
9. Open **Graph** to review the monthly stock trend.
